Answers

FAQ

What is KTL Tech Hub?
KTL Tech Hub is an independent developer brand from India. We build simple, useful Android apps and digital products — including Mantra Japa Counter, Chess Dude, and UploadDude — published on Google Play under the developer name KTL Tech Hub.
Are the apps free or premium?
Apps may offer free access for testing or limited use, with optional premium plans. Mantra Japa Counter is free during closed testing — pricing will be announced at launch. Chess Dude is planned at ₹99/mo, ₹599/yr, or Lifetime ₹3,750 (launch offer ₹1,999). UploadDude pricing will be announced closer to launch.
How do you handle my data and privacy?
We aim to be privacy-first. Local data stays on your device unless you choose optional Google sign-in. See our published privacy policy for what we collect, why, and how to request deletion at contact@ktltechhub.com.
How do refunds work?
Purchases and subscriptions go through Google Play. Refunds and cancellations follow Google Play’s policies — manage them in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions, or request help via Play’s refund flow.
What devices are supported?
Our apps target Android devices via Google Play. Exact OS version requirements are listed on each Play Store listing when the app is available.
How can I contact you?
Email contact@ktltechhub.com or use the contact page. For data deletion related to Google sign-in, use the subject “Delete my data” from your signed-in address.
