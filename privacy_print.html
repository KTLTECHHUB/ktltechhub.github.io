Privacy Policy – Mantra Japa Counter

This privacy policy applies to the Mantra Japa Counter Android app by KTL Tech Hub. For Chess Dude, see the Chess Dude privacy policy at https://ktltechhub.com/privacy-chess-dude.html.

Last updated: 14 November 2026. Developer: KTL Tech Hub (individual developer, India). Contact: contact@ktltechhub.com

1. Information we collect

The app does not use Google sign-in. It does not ask for your name, email address, or Google account, and KTL Tech Hub does not receive those details from the app.

Data on your device: your counts, rounds, streaks, badges, history, custom mantras, language, theme, sound and haptics settings, and reminder settings are stored locally on your device. We do not upload them to our servers. Counting works with no internet connection.

Android backup: if Android backup is turned on, that local chant data and those settings can be copied to your Google account so they can be restored on a new device. Premium subscription status is stored separately on the device and is not included in that backup.

Purchases: optional Premium subscriptions are sold through Google Play Billing at ₹99 per month or ₹599 per year. There is no free trial and no ₹49 offer. We do not receive or store your card or bank details. We only receive purchase status (for example, whether a subscription is active) from Google Play to unlock premium features.

2. Reminders and notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. You can turn them off in the app or in Android settings. Reminder times are not sent to us.

3. How we use information

Only to provide app features, verify premium access, restore your data when Android backup is on, and respond to support requests. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising. The app contains no ads and no third-party analytics.

4. Sharing

We share data with Google only as needed for Google Play Billing, or when required by law. If Android backup is turned on, Android may also copy your local chant data to your Google account. We do not have our own servers for this app, and we do not share your data for advertising. Google's handling of Play purchases and Android backup is described in Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Security and retention

When the app talks to Google Play, that connection is encrypted. Local data stays on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app. A Google backup of your chant data, if one exists, stays in your Google account until you turn backup off or delete it there.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete local data by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app. If Android backup is on, also turn off backup or delete this app's backup in your Google account, so the data is not restored onto a new device. KTL Tech Hub does not keep a copy of your chant data or an account profile on our servers, so there is nothing for us to delete from our side. For questions, email contact@ktltechhub.com. Cancel subscriptions in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions.

7. Children

The app is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help you remove it.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; changes will be posted on this page with a new last-updated date.
